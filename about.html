Leadership & Mill Heritage

Rooted in Bengal's Rich Textile Legacy

Under the visionary stewardship of Founder & CEO Mr. Pankaj Kumar Kiyal, DVP Tex has evolved from a traditional Kolkata trading house into an internationally recognized export manufacturing enterprise.

Bridging Traditional Weaving with Automated Manufacturing

Headquartered at Central Plaza on B.B. Ganguly Street in Bowbazar, Kolkata, DVP Tex operates with a deep respect for natural textile fibers combined with modern automated shuttleless loom technology.

We oversee every phase of production: raw cotton sourcing, spinning, weaving, AZO-free piece dyeing, precision cutting, automated stitching, and strict metal detection. This end-to-end control guarantees our international buyers predictable lead times and uncompromised quality.

Quality Standards

4-Point Fabric Inspection

All greige and dyed fabrics adhere to the globally recognized 4-point grading system. Seam strength, tear resistance, and wash fastness are certified prior to garment sewing.
